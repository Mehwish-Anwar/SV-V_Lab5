# SV-V_Lab5

## Task 1: Identify Operations

| Op_ID | Operation                        |                                     Purpose                                                                           |
| ----- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OP01  | Perform Self-Check               | To check whether all essential sensors and control devices are working correctly. |
| OP02  | Record Artifact Information      | To record the artifact identification information.                                |
| OP03  | Load Environmental Profile       | To load the required environmental limits of the artifact.                        |
| OP04  | Monitor Environmental Conditions | To continuously monitor temperature, humidity, light and other conditions.        |
| OP05  | Verify Door Status               | To check whether the chamber door is open or closed.                              |
| OP06  | Activate Conservation Controls   | To start normal environmental control for the artifact.                           |
| OP07  | Correct Temperature              | To bring the temperature back within the permitted range.                         |
| OP08  | Correct Humidity                 | To bring the humidity back within the permitted range.                            |
| OP09  | Verify Environmental Recovery    | To confirm that the environment has returned to the permitted range.              |
| OP10  | Activate Protection Measures     | To protect the artifact when an environmental condition cannot be corrected.      |
| OP11  | Generate Operator Alert          | To inform the museum operator about an abnormal condition.                        |
| OP12  | Detect Vibration                 | To detect significant vibration inside the chamber.                               |
| OP13  | Verify Vibration Stabilization   | To confirm that vibration remains below the safe threshold for the required time. |
| OP14  | Suspend Conservation Activities  | To stop normal conservation activities when the door is open or vibration occurs. |
| OP15  | Switch to Emergency Power        | To use emergency power when the main power is lost.                               |
| OP16  | Record Power Failure             | To record a power failure when emergency power is unavailable.                    |
| OP17  | Perform Safe Shutdown            | To safely shut down the chamber when power cannot be restored.                    |
| OP18  | Verify Safe Artifact Removal     | To confirm that the chamber is safe before the artifact is removed.               |
