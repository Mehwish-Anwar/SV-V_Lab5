## Task 2: Complete Operation Schema

| **ID** | **Operation**                    | **Pre-condition**                                                               | **Input**                                    | **Post-condition**                                                                     |
| ------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OP01   | Perform Self-Check               | Chamber is powered on.                                                          | Sensor and control-device status             | Essential sensors and devices are verified as working.                                 |
| OP02   | Record Artifact Information      | Artifact is placed inside the chamber.                                          | Artifact ID and identification details       | Artifact information is stored in the system.                                          |
| OP03   | Load Environmental Profile       | Artifact information is available.                                              | Temperature and humidity limits              | Required environmental limits are loaded.                                              |
| OP04   | Monitor Environmental Conditions | System is powered and sensors are working.                                      | Sensor readings                              | Environmental conditions are continuously monitored.                                   |
| OP05   | Verify Door Status               | Door sensor is working.                                                         | Door sensor reading                          | Door status is confirmed as open or closed.                                            |
| OP06   | Activate Conservation Controls   | Artifact profile is loaded and door is closed.                                  | Environmental profile                        | Normal environmental controls are activated.                                           |
| OP07   | Correct Temperature              | Temperature is outside the permitted range.                                     | Current temperature and required range       | Temperature correction command is issued.                                              |
| OP08   | Correct Humidity                 | Humidity is outside the permitted range.                                        | Current humidity and required range          | Humidity correction command is issued.                                                 |
| OP09   | Verify Environmental Recovery    | Temperature or humidity correction has been attempted.                          | Updated sensor readings                      | System confirms whether the condition has returned to the permitted range.             |
| OP10   | Activate Protection Measures     | Environmental condition cannot be corrected within the allowed recovery period. | Environmental fault information              | Protection measures are activated.                                                     |
| OP11   | Generate Operator Alert          | An abnormal or dangerous condition exists.                                      | Fault information                            | Alert is generated for the museum operator.                                            |
| OP12   | Detect Vibration                 | Artifact is inside and vibration sensor is working.                             | Vibration sensor reading                     | Significant vibration is detected or normal vibration is confirmed.                    |
| OP13   | Verify Vibration Stabilization   | Significant vibration has stopped.                                              | Vibration readings and stabilization time    | System confirms that vibration is below the permitted threshold for the required time. |
| OP14   | Suspend Conservation Activities  | Door is opened or significant vibration is detected.                            | Door status or vibration information         | Normal conservation activities are suspended.                                          |
| OP15   | Switch to Emergency Power        | Main power is unavailable and emergency power is available.                     | Power availability status                    | System switches to emergency power.                                                    |
| OP16   | Record Power Failure             | Main and emergency power are unavailable.                                       | Power failure information                    | Power failure incident is recorded.                                                    |
| OP17   | Perform Safe Shutdown            | Main and emergency power are unavailable.                                       | Power status                                 | System enters a safe shutdown condition.                                               |
| OP18   | Verify Safe Artifact Removal     | Operator requests artifact removal.                                             | Chamber, protection and environmental status | System confirms whether the artifact can be safely removed.                            |
